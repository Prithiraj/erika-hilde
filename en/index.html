Why here?

Not another bar trying to look like every other bar.

The strongest story is already here: a long-documented neighbourhood bar directly by the Neukölln Ship Canal, with a warm lived-in room and a public reputation for a relaxed atmosphere.

Current guest sentiment repeatedly points to the canal location, friendly service, fair prices and an easygoing mood. That is the positioning — not cocktail theatre.

The room

A place does not need to look “new” to feel exactly right.

What is publicly visible and described is concrete: table lamps, newspapers, mixed seating, a warm interior, and an atmosphere where someone can simply read if they arrive early.

The product is not the décor. It is the feeling that you are allowed to stay.

From the archive

Documented on Weigandufer by 2010.

A Berlin food blog described Erika & Hilde in May 2010 as a young Franconian/Swabian pub with Brotzeit, homemade food, cake and beer from Wettelsheim. That is history — not today’s menu.

Meeting place

Berlin Writers’ Workshop repeatedly used Erika & Hilde for open Stammtisch gatherings in 2024, 2025 and 2026. Some event descriptions explicitly mention the back room.

These are third-party groups choosing the venue, not events claimed to be operated by Erika & Hilde.
